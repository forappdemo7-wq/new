# Legal Compliance & Licensing Policy

**get-now Open Library** is an ad-free, privacy-first, non-profit digital archive. We maintain an uncompromising standard of copyright compliance. Every work hosted or cataloged on this platform must pass strict legal criteria.

---

## 1. The Core Legal Principles

1. **Dual Licensing Tracking**:
   The license of a book's **text/translation** is tracked **independently** from its **cover illustration**. A 19th-century public domain novel may feature a contemporary copyrighted cover illustration. We never assume a cover is open because the text is open. If a cover license is unknown, we generate an automated typographic **Ghost Cover**.

2. **Jurisdiction-Aware Cataloging**:
   Copyright terms vary by country. We record the specific legal jurisdiction for every work:
   - `WORLDWIDE_PD`: Works published before 1929 where the author passed away over 70 years ago, or explicit CC0 dedications.
   - `PD_US_ONLY`: Works published in the US between 1929 and 1977 without copyright notice or without copyright renewal. These are **geofenced** and hidden from global catalogs by default.
   - `PD_JAPAN_ONLY`: Vintage Japanese works published prior to 1953 under Japan's historical 50-year post-mortem rule.
   - `CC_BY` / `CC_BY_SA` / `CC_BY_NC`: Verified Creative Commons licensed original creations.
   - `LINK_OUT_ONLY`: Free-to-read legal works hosted on external authorized repositories that we do not host directly.

---

## 2. Ingestion Checklist for New Works

Before any work is marked as `published`, it must satisfy all 5 checkpoints:

- [ ] **1. Public Domain Date Check**:
  - Published before **January 1, 1929** (US Public Domain).
  - AND author died at least **70 years ago** (Life + 70 rule for EU/UK/Commonwealth).
  - *OR* published in Japan before **1953** (Japanese vintage rule).
- [ ] **2. Creative Commons Verification**:
  - An explicit license badge or statement exists from the creator (e.g., CC0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0).
  - The source repository URL is permanently recorded.
- [ ] **3. Translation & Derivative Check**:
  - If the work is a translation of a classic text (e.g., Dante or Homer), the *translator's* copyright must also be expired or openly licensed.
- [ ] **4. Cover Art Provenance**:
  - Cover art has a verified source (Open Library, Digital Comic Museum, Wikimedia Commons, or Original Vector Art).
  - No copyrighted modern dust jackets or movie tie-in covers are used.
- [ ] **5. Source Accessibility & Integrity**:
  - Download URLs (EPUB, PDF, CBZ) point to legitimate non-profit preservation repositories (Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Internet Archive, Smithsonian, Digital Comic Museum).

---

## 3. DMCA & Rights-Holder Protocol

We respond to all DMCA and rights-holder notices within **24 hours**. In the event of a valid takedown notice or jurisdiction challenge:
1. The work is immediately placed in `quarantine` status and removed from public access.
2. The audit log is updated with the case number, claimant statement, and resolution.
3. If confirmed infringing, the record is permanently deleted and blacklisted from re-ingestion.
